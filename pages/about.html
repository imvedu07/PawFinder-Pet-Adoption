ABOUT PAWFINDER

More than just adoption.

PawFinder was created with one simple idea: every pet deserves the chance to find a loving home, and every person deserves the joy of finding a loyal companion.

We bring pets, families and shelters together through a simple and friendly adoption experience.

OUR MISSION

Give every pet a place to belong.

Many wonderful animals are waiting for someone to give them a second chance. PawFinder makes it easier for people to discover these pets and understand what adoption involves.

Our goal is to make responsible pet adoption simple, transparent and accessible while helping shelters connect with caring families.

WHAT WE BELIEVE

Built around three things.

Compassion

Every animal deserves kindness, patience and the opportunity to live in a safe and loving environment.

Responsible Homes

We encourage thoughtful adoption by helping people understand the commitment of welcoming a pet into their home.

Community

Shelters, volunteers, families and animal lovers can work together to create better futures for pets.
